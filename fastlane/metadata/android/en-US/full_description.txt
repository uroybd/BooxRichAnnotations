Boox Rich Annotation extracts and exports the highlights, notes, and annotations you make in the NeoReader app on Onyx Boox e-readers, so you can back them up or move them into another tool.

Requires an Onyx Boox device with the NeoReader app installed; it reads annotation data directly from NeoReader's on-device content provider.

Features:

* Browse your library of books (EPUB, MOBI, AZW/AZW3) in one place
* Search books instantly by title or author
* Browse by book, or see every annotation from every book grouped under collapsible book headers
* View each annotation as its own card, with style, page, chapter, timestamp, quote, and note
* Select individual annotations, a whole book, or everything across multiple books, then export or share just that selection
* Export annotations with colors, styles, and notes preserved
* Export to JSON, CSV, or a customizable text format (Markdown by default), including multi-book exports
* Create custom export templates with the Pebble templating engine, with syntax highlighting in the editor
* Choose any folder on your device to save exported files
* Configure a default export format and text template in preferences
* Automatically deduplicates annotations that were edited more than once
* Pure black-and-white theme with zero animations, designed for e-ink displays
